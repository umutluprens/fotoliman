# FotoLiman

FotoLiman backs up your photos and videos to your own disk and verifies every copy. It works with Google Photos, your phone (by cable or Wi-Fi QR) and camera or SD cards, on Mac and Windows.

**Download:** [English](https://umutluprens.github.io/fotoliman/download/) or [Türkçe](https://umutluprens.github.io/fotoliman/indir/)<br>
**Latest version:** [1.2.4.3](https://github.com/umutluprens/fotoliman/releases/latest)

This repository holds the FotoLiman website (download, privacy and terms pages) and the public release notes. The source code of the app is kept in a separate private repository.

### Requirements

- Mac with Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later
- Windows 10 or 11, x64 or ARM64 (Snapdragon)
- FotoLiman Mobile for Android is installed from the download page as an APK

The app is not signed, so Windows shows a SmartScreen warning on first launch. Choose More info and then Run anyway.

### Privacy

FotoLiman copies files from your sources to a disk you choose. It does not upload your photos anywhere and sends no information about your computer or your photos. See the [privacy policy](https://umutluprens.github.io/fotoliman/privacy.html) and [terms of use](https://umutluprens.github.io/fotoliman/terms.html).

## Türkçe

### Gereksinimler

- Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 ve üstü
- Windows 10 veya 11, x64 ya da ARM64 (Snapdragon)
